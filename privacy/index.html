Privacy Policy — Qroozenstern Board Organizer

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Qroozenstern Board Organizer is a personal tool used solely by its owner to organize their own Pinterest boards and pins.

  • It accesses only the owner's own Pinterest account data (boards and pins).
  • No data is collected from other users.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Data is processed only to reorganize the owner's boards and is not stored beyond what is needed for that task.
